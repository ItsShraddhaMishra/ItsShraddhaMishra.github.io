05 2023/11 — 2026/11

Research Assistant · Academia Sinica, Taiwan

Laboratory: Advanced Cryptographic Engineering (ACE) Lab · Post-Quantum Cryptography Laboratory Group · Institute of Information Science (IIS)

DL-SCA · Research Infrastructure · Probabilistic Evaluation · Reproducibility

At Academia Sinica, deep-learning side-channel analysis became my schooling in research method. A DL-SCA model outputs a probability distribution over key-dependent values, and its success lies in how that evidence accumulates across traces, measured by guessing entropy, key rank and the number of traces needed to recover the key, rather than by accuracy. Models with near-random accuracy can still recover the key, and results shift with the data split, the seed and the attack budget. I learned to judge probabilistic models through rank-based, test-trace budget-aware metrics and through variance across repeated runs. Because published results proved difficult to reproduce, I built TraceFlow, and I will carry its practice into my doctoral research: configuration as code, traceable runs, provenance, tamper-evident artifacts for verifiable and auditable results, and faithful reruns.

06 2026/11 — Present

Towards a PhD · Independent Preparation, Taiwan

Decision-Making under Uncertainty · Engineering Mathematics · Risk-Aware Learning · Multi-Agent Systems

One question has run through all of my work: how can a learning system arrive at the best possible decision, and how can we trust that it did? In my master’s research, I pursued it at the level of the model, searching for the circuit architecture that decided best from image data. At Academia Sinica, I pursued it at the level of the evidence, building infrastructure that makes research decisions reproducible and verifiable at scale. In my PhD, I intend to pursue it at the level of the decision itself: autonomous, risk-aware and reliable decision-making among adaptive and adversarial agents under regime shift. Financial markets will be my most demanding testbed, not the boundary of the work. In preparation, I am strengthening my foundation in engineering mathematics and studying the methods on which such decisions rest.
